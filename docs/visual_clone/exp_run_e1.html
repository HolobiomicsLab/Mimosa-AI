run_e1 — E1 rubric surgery

E1 asked whether re-aggregating the already-parsed claim verdicts can fix the verifier's inversion at the top of its ranking. run_e1.py rescores 607 frozen examples under 15 static re-aggregations plus two fitted baselines. Best observed move is +0.014 AUC_sr against a gate of +0.05, so every variant fails.
