e13_l3_extreme — manifold and capacity study

E13 asked whether the linear claim readout (L3) leaves structure unused: a deeper claim manifold, set attention, hashed pairwise terms, soft judge targets, base rates, or goal conditioning. Five arms answered it on cached embeddings, with no LLM calls. Nothing beat L3 by more than the +0.02 gate, two FiLM arms passed the numeric gate but shipped the same 35 tasks as the recorded reward.

Why it matters: two arms cleared the numeric gate and still moved no pick, so between-task AUC and within-task picking are separate questions. That result is what closed the capacity line and pointed E14 at a tournament instead.

Mismatch to state: the gate rule compares pooled AUC and a cluster-bootstrap CI, while the acceptance test is the E9 replay pick count. Both FiLM arms pass the first and tie the second, so the gate and the acceptance test do not agree on this pair of arms (md L22).
