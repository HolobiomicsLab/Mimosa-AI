e16_gradient_audit — does the gradient point at gold?

E16 asked one question: when the verifier writes a textual gradient for generation N, does that feedback point at the properties the benchmark's gold program measures? It built a population of 577 gradient-bearing generations and validated a judge instrument against mutated controls. It then measured alignment on 565 gradients and the correlations with reward and SR; the reward line itself is scored by the hybrid verifier.
