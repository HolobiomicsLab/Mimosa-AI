e13_umap — claim manifold coloured by learned weight

E13's arm a asked whether the learned claim weight w(c) varies smoothly over the claim-embedding space, which would let a clustered readout beat linear L3. This script draws that space in two dimensions and measures how much w(c) agrees with its nearest neighbours. The answer is a smooth layout with r = 0.8922 in 2-D and r = 0.9492 in the original 384 dimensions, and the piecewise readout still lost to L3.

Why it matters: 384-d coherence (0.9492) is higher than 2-D coherence (0.8922), so the layout barely loses the smoothness it measures; a smooth w(c) is still not enough to beat linear L3, which is what exp_e13_l3_extreme.html reports (piecewise Δ -0.0607).

Amber: the figure is a 2-D visualisation only; the quantitative test of the manifold idea is the paired piecewise-versus-global AUC in arm a, not this correlation (stated as a gap in E13_l3_extreme_2026-09-23.md L36).
