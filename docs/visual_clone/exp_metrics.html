metrics.py — the shared section-0 metric library

This file is the section-0 metric contract of the verifier-search harness: AUC against SR and VER, Brier, Kendall tau overall and in the top score quartile, within-task ranking accuracy, exact McNemar, and two task-cluster bootstraps. Two scripts call it, run_e1.py and run_e2.py, and every number they publish is labelled "my computation" over frozen claim verdicts. The Bradley–Terry scripts (E15, E18, E19) never import it; they re-implement the fit and the bootstrap, and the ledgers named in the evidence map come from those scripts instead.
