e4_policy_replay — E4 policy replay

E4 asked which selection policy over already-graded generations reaches the oracle with the fewest grader queries. e4_policy_replay.py replays 10 policies over 207 tasks and 1 063 shippable generations with zero LLM calls. Re-grading the top-3 by reward reaches 101 tasks against oracle 102 and current pick P0 91, at 1.96 queries per task.
