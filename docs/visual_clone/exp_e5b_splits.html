e5b_splits — is the LOTO 0.753 a lucky fold?

E5 reported a leave-one-task-out AUC of 0.753 for its winning rung L3 (MiniLM(name+desc) → logistic). This experiment repeats the split war 40 times per unit, 25 for the campaign holdout, with the same data and seeds, and evaluates three baselines on the identical held-out side. Folder context: data_experiments.html.

Amber: the campaign-level holdout draws from 5 campaigns only, and asb_full_iterative_1 holds 448 of 607 examples, so those seeds mostly measure whether that one campaign was held out. The .md labels it indicative (L62).

Amber: main protocol fixes C=0.1; E5's 0.753 used inner-CV C. The script states this and recomputes the anchor under both policies rather than comparing blind (.md L70).

Why it matters: the example-level number is optimistic by design — the same task workspaces sit on both sides of a leaky split.
