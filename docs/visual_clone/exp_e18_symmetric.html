e18_symmetric — A/B label-swap re-aggregation

This follow-up asked whether the E18 Bradley–Terry ranking comes from the artifacts or from the A/B label order. It re-executed the 599 cached comparison scripts with the candidate inputs swapped, with zero new LLM calls. Agreement keeps the winner, disagreement becomes a tie.
