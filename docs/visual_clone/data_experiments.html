Experiment trees

Two experiment folders sit beside the code: experiments_verifiers/ (offline verifier research on frozen campaign data) and diversity_experiments/ (diversity-metric comparison on the same runs). Both read evidence that data_runs.html describes, and neither is imported by the shipped package. Numbers marked "my computation" come from du -sk, find and key counts run on 2026-10-02.

Diversity experiments

Diagram 4 - diversity_experiments/ layout. Counts are my computation (2026-10-02); "runs" counts copied state_result.json files, not directories.

The folder compares candidate behaviour descriptors for the QD archive, per campaign, against the descriptors Mimosa ships. README.md lists the 5 metrics and the 4 scripts; the plan behind the current descriptor is mod_core_selection.html, and the writer of the archive rows is sources/core/evolution_engine.py::EvolutionEngine._persist_qd_archive.

From diversity_experiments/report.html, TL;DR block: 5 descriptors x 5 campaigns, 1 141 workflow uuids, 23 computable metric x folder runs. Actions embedding reaches 2x the within-task resolution of genotype (pooled 0.132 vs 0.095) with a within/cross ratio of 0.44. The deprecated fingerprint shows 12.3% exact duplicate vectors on average (24% in asb_iterative_1) and admits almost every candidate (1-6 rejections, where genotype rejects 193/708). Genotype coverage is 0.77 mean and 0.4% duplicates.

My count of the copied tree differs from the report: 1 127 uuid directories on disk, 956 of them non-empty; the report counts the source uuids before copying.

Archive rows come from qd_archive.jsonl (1 copied row, 14 keys incl. qd_descriptor, qd_score, evicted_uuid) and variation_log.jsonl (22 rows, 3 schema variants). Both are written by sources/core/evolution_engine.py::EvolutionEngine._persist_qd_archive and ::_persist_variation_log; see data_runs.html.
